# Licence of the pretrained weights

The source code in this repository is licensed under MIT (see `LICENSE`). The pretrained weights
(`dronedet-*.pth`, distributed as GitHub release assets) are licensed differently, because they were
trained on datasets with their own terms.

The weights are licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
licence** (CC BY-NC 4.0), <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>. This additional
restriction also applies:

> The weights may not be used for military, defence, intelligence, security, weapons or
> surveillance applications.

The restriction comes from the licence of the DroneAudioDataset, one of the training sets. The
non-commercial term follows the CC BY-NC licences of UrbanSound8K and ESC-50.

To train weights without these restrictions, use the code (MIT) on data whose licence allows your
use.
